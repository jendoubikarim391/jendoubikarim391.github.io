AI chatbot and automation demos

Two working demos for a fictional holiday rental (Casa Ginestra). They use sample data and fixed rules so they run in a single page. Real projects use an AI model and connect to your own inbox, calendar and booking sheet. By Karim J., Tuscany, Italy.

Guest AssistantA chatbot that answers guests in Italian, English, Ukrainian, Russian, German and French, with a host view of what was asked. Lead DeskGuest enquiries are sorted, checked for duplicates and answered with a draft in the guest's language. A person approves before anything is sent.
